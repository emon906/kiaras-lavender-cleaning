### 3. Required Sections

**1. Ultra-Luxury Hero Banner**
Create a spectacular, full-width hero with a premium editorial layout. Use a beautiful luxury home-cleaning image, elegant purple gradient overlays, glowing gold details, and sophisticated decorative elements. Add a large headline: **“A Spotless Home. A More Beautiful Life.”** Include the business name, Georgia & Chattanooga service area, prominent phone number, “Get a Free Estimate” button, and “Now Booking” badge. Make this the most visually impressive section. No background video.

**2. About Kiara**
Create an elegant personal introduction section with a premium portrait/image layout and editable biography content. Do not invent personal history.

**3. Our Premium Services**
Showcase all services in an expensive-looking editorial grid:
- Standard Cleaning
- Deep Cleaning (Walls, Baseboards & More)
- Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning
- Salon Cleaning
- Garage Cleaning
- Personal Gym Cleaning
- Weekly & Bi-Weekly Plans
- Laundry Services (Pickup or Drop-Off)
- Couch Cleaning (Starting at $100)
- Caulking (Showers & Tubs)
- Small Hole Repair (Wall Patch-Ups)
- Carpet Cleaning

Carpet Pricing:
- 1 Room — $50
- 2 Rooms — $80
- 3 or More Rooms — $120

**4. Why Choose Kiara’s**
Create a premium asymmetric section highlighting reliability, attention to detail, flexible scheduling, professional service, and customer satisfaction. Use elegant icons and refined animations.

**5. Welcome Clean Offer**
Design a luxurious promotional section for first-time clients featuring the supplied Welcome Clean offer for the first week of service. Add a strong booking CTA.

**6. Premium Image Gallery**
Create a beautiful masonry-style gallery with 8 high-quality images of spotless kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living rooms, salons, and cleaning transformations. Include elegant hover effects and image lightbox viewing.

**7. Before & After**
Add a visually impressive interactive before-and-after comparison slider using realistic cleaning transformation photos. Make it touch-friendly and responsive.

**8. Ultra-Luxury Testimonials**
Create an exceptional testimonial section with an editorial layout, elegant quote typography, premium purple-and-gold styling, and a smooth carousel. Use genuine customer reviews only. Never invent customer names, reviews, or five-star ratings. If reviews are unavailable, provide editable placeholders.

**9. Cleaning Reels — Exactly 3**
Create a separate premium Instagram-style vertical video section with exactly **3 reel cards**. Use vertical 9:16 video frames, smooth mouse-wheel scrolling, snap transitions, mobile swipe support, play/pause controls, and automatic pause when videos leave the viewport. Add editable video URLs. Do not create more than three reels.

**10. Service Area & Interactive Map**
Add a beautiful interactive map focused on Georgia and Chattanooga, USA. Use verified service-area locations, elegant custom purple markers, and a premium information panel. Do not invent an exact street address.

**11. Payment Options**
Show Tap to Pay, Cash App, Venmo, Chime, and email/text invoices in an elegant visual layout.

**12. Contact & Booking**
Create a luxurious contact section with clickable phone number `(423) 287-7090`, service information, and a functional estimate-request form.

**13. Premium Footer**
Include logo, navigation, service links, contact information, service areas, and a beautiful final CTA.

### 4. Design & Technical Requirements
- Use React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, and existing project components.
- Use large, readable typography and generous spacing.
- Make every section visually unique; never repeat the same card layout throughout the website.
- Add smooth scroll reveals, refined hover interactions, subtle parallax, and premium transitions.
- Ensure responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Optimise images and videos for fast loading.
- Make the map, gallery, before/after slider, exactly 3 reels, buttons, and contact form functional.
- Keep business details, pricing, gallery, video URLs, testimonials, and map settings editable.
- Do not invent reviews, credentials, exact addresses, or additional prices.
- Reuse existing dependencies wherever possible.

**IMPORTANT:** Do not create a basic template. The hero must look spectacular, the testimonials must feel ultra-luxurious, and the entire website should have a consistent high-end purple, pink, and gold aesthetic.

**Final Goal:** Build an unforgettable, ultra-premium cleaning service website that makes Kiara’s Quality Cleaning Service look like an established luxury brand and encourages visitors to book immediately.
